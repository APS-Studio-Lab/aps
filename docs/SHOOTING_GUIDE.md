# Shooting guide: phone + green screen

The pipeline can fix uneven exposure and colour between takes, and it can upscale to 4K. It cannot add detail
that was never recorded, so these settings matter more than anything done in post.

## Camera (phone, rear main camera)
- **4K at 30 fps.** This is the single biggest quality jump: the vertical short is a crop of the frame, and 4K
  lets the crop stay sharp.
- **HDR video off** (record SDR). HDR is converted correctly, but SDR keys and grades more predictably.
- **Lock exposure and focus** (long-press on your face) so brightness doesn't pump between takes.
- If your camera app allows it: shutter 1/60, ISO as low as possible, white balance fixed at about 4500K.
- Highest bitrate setting if available (iPhone: "Most Compatible" off; Android: "High bitrate video" on).
- Frame from mid-chest up with headroom; camera lens at eye level, 1.5–2 m from you.

## Green screen
- **Iron or stretch it.** Wrinkles create shadows the keyer has to work around.
- Light the screen **separately and evenly** (two lights or a big window), and stand **at least 1.5 m in front of it**
  so no green light spills onto you.
- No green clothing; avoid very thin, flyaway edges where possible.

## Lighting you
- One soft key light at 45° to **your left** (matches the default set, where the lamp is on the left).
- A dimmer fill on the right, or none for a more dramatic look.
- Keep the same positions every session; take a phone photo of the setup to repeat it.

## Delivery
- Use a **teleprompter** so you don't look down between lines. Retakes are fine: the pipeline keeps the last
  attempt of a repeated line automatically.
- Clap once at the start of each recording; it makes voice sync even more reliable.
- Record the lapel mic as a separate file and upload it next to the video.
